# LinkedIn Voice, Tone & Formatting

Read this before writing any post. These rules apply to every format. Layer the user's own **Tone**, **Words-to-love**, and **Words-to-avoid** from `~/.claude/brand-profile.md` on top of everything here.

---

## Voice & Tone

- Direct and self-assured. Matter-of-fact about results, not humble-braggy.
- First-person always. "I", "my", "here's what I did."
- Specific > generic. "195 posts" not "a lot of posts."
- Casual but sharp. Contractions fine. "I packaged it" not "I have compiled."
- Anti-corporate. No "leverage synergies", "actionable insights", "game-changer."
- Occasionally raw and human — a little edge is on-brand if it fits the user's tone.
- Generous framing: give things away. CTAs offer value, not demands.

**Simple language.** Short words. Short sentences. If a sentence has more than 10 words, break it in two. No jargon. No acronyms. If a 10-year-old would need to look it up, rewrite it.

**Use the audience's language verbatim when available.** Pull exact phrases from the brand profile's Main-pain and Audience notes. The reader should see a hook and think "that's exactly how I'd say it."

**No internal tool names in the post body.** The names of the user's private tools, apps, or vendors mean nothing to someone who isn't already a customer. Replace with plain descriptions: "a tool that finds what's already working," "an AI trained on my voice," "one workflow that posts everywhere."

---

## Formatting Rules

- `→` for positive bullet lists
- `𐄂` for negative/didn't-do lists
- `1/`, `2/`, `3/` for numbered steps (not `1.`)
- 1-3 sentences per paragraph. Most are 1.
- Blank line between every paragraph
- No headers, no bold, no markdown in the post body
- CTA keyword in quotes: `Comment "KEYWORD" and I'll send you the link.`

---

## Hook Rules

- One sentence. Two max.
- Specific number, dollar amount, timeframe, or result. Never vague.
- First person ("I") — never third-person, never a quote.
- No ellipsis, no exclamation marks. Confident, not hyped.
- Only use numbers the user can verify (from Proof/wins). Never fabricate a stat.

---

## Proof Anchors

Use at least one in every post, drawn from the brand profile's **Proof/wins** and **Credentials**:

**Primary (the user's results):**
A one-line stack of their strongest, verifiable numbers, e.g. `[followers/revenue/result]. This is the system.`

**Cost anchor (only if the profile documents one and the post involves tooling costs):**
`Total cost: [X]/day.` or `[N] AI agents handling all of it for [X]/day.`

**Vary the framing, keep the numbers accurate.**

---

## Objection Removers

Embed inside the arrow list — never in a separate section:
`No [expensive thing]. No [time thing]. Just [simple thing].`

Examples:
- `No code. No tech background. Just AI doing the grunt work.`
- `No agency. No designer. No expensive tools.`
- `No employees. No cold outreach. No 60-hour weeks.`

---

## Output Format

Write the full post, ready to copy-paste. No preamble, no "here's your post:" — just the post.

After the post, add:
```
---
Hook formula used: [pattern name] — for A/B tracking. Strip before publishing.
```
