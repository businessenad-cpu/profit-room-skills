# Reference Posts (structural examples)

These are generic skeletons that show the rhythm, sentence length, hook structure, and CTA format — not real posts. Study the *shape*, then write in the user's own voice with their real numbers from the brand profile. Everything in brackets is a placeholder.

## Key Patterns to Notice

- Hook is always 1 line, always has a number or a transformation
- "Here's X:" is a recurring setup line that creates a scroll stop
- Body paragraphs are 1-3 sentences, often just 1
- Arrow lists use `→`, negative lists use `𐄂`
- The CTA keyword is always in CAPS or quotes
- Posts end with a PS (see PS Rules) or a simple "must be connected" qualifier
- Never ask people to follow — give value and let the post do the work
