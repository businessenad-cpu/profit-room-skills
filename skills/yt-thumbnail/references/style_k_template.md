# Style K — Whiteboard Number

## Description
Person on the LEFT, serious or intense expression. A large physical whiteboard fills the RIGHT 2/3 of the frame — one enormous handwritten number or word in marker on it. Light background overall. No bottom bar, no text overlay — the whiteboard IS the message. Raw, credible, teaching energy without the studio feel.

Reference format: a single giant handwritten number on a physical whiteboard.

## Full Prompt Template

```
A photorealistic, high-resolution YouTube thumbnail. WIDE 16:9 — never portrait.

**Background:** Natural, slightly warm off-white or light grey background. The room behind is softly visible but not distracting — plain wall, neutral tone.

**Person (LEFT side of frame):**
Use Image 1 for exact likeness — match the person's hair, facial hair, glasses, and clothing from your reference photo. Positioned in the LEFT 30-35% of the frame. Chest-up. [INSERT EXPRESSION — serious, intense, contemplative — NOT smiling. e.g., "arms folded, direct stare toward camera" OR "looking slightly toward the whiteboard, thoughtful"]. The person should feel like they're presenting something real.

**Whiteboard (RIGHT 60-65% of frame):**
A large physical whiteboard — white surface, aluminum or wooden frame. It stands behind and beside the person, tilted very slightly toward camera. On its surface, in thick black or dark marker:

[INSERT WHITEBOARD CONTENT — one dominant element + optional supporting text]:
- ONE very large handwritten number or word — fills most of the whiteboard surface, e.g., "$100,000" or "$8,947" or "PASSIVE"
- Optional: one or two smaller supporting words below in smaller marker text, e.g., "/month" or "no audience"
- Optional: a simple underline or emphasis mark beneath the main number

Handwriting style: confident, large, slightly imperfect — like someone actually wrote it with a marker. Not computer font. Not clipart. Real marker energy.

**Lighting:** Bright, natural, even. The whiteboard reads clean white. Person is well-lit from the front. Light and approachable — not cinematic or moody.

**No text overlay** — the whiteboard content IS the only text in the image. Do not add any other typography.

**CRITICAL:** Wide 16:9. Person LEFT third. Whiteboard RIGHT two-thirds. Light overall. One huge handwritten number/word on whiteboard. Nothing else.
```

## Placeholder Guide

### [INSERT WHITEBOARD CONTENT]
The whiteboard carries the entire message — one big proof point or hook:
- "$8,947" with "/earned" smaller below
- "$2,500/mo" with "passive" smaller below
- "NO AUDIENCE" in large marker
- A single formula: "Claude + Stripe = $9K"

### [INSERT EXPRESSION]
- Arms folded, direct stare — "I know something you don't"
- Looking toward whiteboard, slightly away — teaching/presenting energy
- Hands clasped, leaning slightly forward — serious, direct

## When to Use
- Proof/outcome videos where credibility is the hook
- Educational videos with a single big insight or number
- When you want light background but still face-forward personal brand feel
- Pairs with: Proof title format, Parenthetical, Confession

## Notes
- A bright-colored top works well here — it pops against the white whiteboard
- Keep the whiteboard content to ONE primary element — the power is in the simplicity
- If the number is the hook, expression should be serious — not excited, not smiling
