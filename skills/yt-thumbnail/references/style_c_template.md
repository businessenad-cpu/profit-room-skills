# Style C — Cinematic Dual-Hold

## Description
Photorealistic YouTube thumbnail. You hold one large 3D glowing app icon in each hand, arms outstretched toward camera. Dark cinematic bokeh background. Each icon casts its own color as a split light source onto your face and hoodie. No text in the image — the icons ARE the message.

## Full Prompt Template

```
A photorealistic, cinematic, high-resolution 16:9 YouTube thumbnail at 3840x2160. The image must be wider than it is tall — landscape orientation only.

**Subject:** The person from your reference photo (Image 1) — match their exact likeness (hair, facial hair, glasses, and clothing). They are smiling directly at the camera with a confident, enthusiastic expression. They are centered in the frame, chest-up shot, filling approximately 60% of the frame height.

**Pose:** Both arms are extended outward toward the camera, each hand holding up one large oversized app icon — one in each hand — like they are presenting them. The icons are large enough to be clearly identifiable as distinct app icons (roughly the size of their head each). Their arms are slightly bent, palms facing the camera, icons angled slightly toward the viewer.

**Left hand icon:** [INSERT LEFT ICON DESCRIPTION — e.g., "A large rounded-square 3D app icon in warm orange/terracotta color. The icon has the Claude Anthropic symbol — two black rectangular eye cutouts on an orange face. It emits a warm orange-amber glow and casts warm orange light onto the left side of your face and hoodie."]

**Right hand icon:** [INSERT RIGHT ICON DESCRIPTION — e.g., "A large rounded-square 3D app icon in white and cool blue. The icon shows the Arco 'A' arch logo in gradient blue/orange. It emits a cool blue-white glow and casts cool blue light onto the right side of your face and hand."]

**Dual split lighting:** This is critical. The left icon acts as a warm-colored light source illuminating your left side. The right icon acts as a cool-colored light source illuminating your right side. The result is a dramatic split-lighting effect — warm on the left, cool on the right — meeting in the middle of their face and chest.

**Background:** A dark, out-of-focus room or studio. Soft bokeh. Warm and cool tones visible in the blurred background, echoing the icon colors. No visible text, logos, or distracting elements in the background.

**Photography style:** Studio portrait photography. 85mm equivalent focal length. Shallow depth of field — subject sharp, background completely blurred. Professional retouched photo quality. Natural skin tones. High contrast. Cinematic color grade.

**CRITICAL:** The output MUST be a wide landscape 16:9 image (wider than it is tall). This is a YouTube thumbnail — never portrait orientation. The person should be centered with the two icons flanking them on each side.
```

## Placeholder Guide

### [INSERT LEFT ICON DESCRIPTION]
Describe:
1. **Shape**: Always "large rounded-square 3D app icon"
2. **Color/material**: The dominant color of the tool's brand. Can be glossy, frosted, matte.
3. **Symbol**: What's on the icon face — described from the logo file or brand knowledge
4. **Glow color**: What color light does this icon emit? Should match the tool's brand color.
5. **Lighting effect**: "casts [color] light onto the left side of your face and hoodie"

Example fills:
- Claude: "warm orange-terracotta frosted glass icon. The face shows two black rectangular eye cutouts — the Claude anthropic logo symbol. It emits a warm amber-orange glow and casts warm orange light onto the left side of your face and hoodie."
- n8n: "dark rounded-square icon with the bright orange n8n logo (a stylized 'n8n' wordmark). It emits a warm orange-red glow and casts orange light onto the left side of your face."
- Notion: "clean white rounded-square icon with a large black Notion 'N' logo. It emits a soft white-yellow glow and casts warm white light onto the left side of your face."
- ChatGPT: "dark teal/black rounded-square icon with the white ChatGPT swirl logo. It emits a cool teal-green glow."

### [INSERT RIGHT ICON DESCRIPTION]
Same structure as left, but for the right hand. Use a contrasting color temperature (if left is warm, make right cool — or vice versa) to maximize the split-lighting drama.

## Icon Color Pairing Guide

Pick pairs with strong color contrast for maximum visual impact:

| Left (warm) | Right (cool) |
|-------------|-------------|
| Claude (orange) | Cursor/Arco (blue/white) |
| n8n (orange) | ChatGPT (teal) |
| Make (purple-warm) | Notion (white/cool) |
| Gemini (gradient warm) | Claude Code (cool blue) |
| ElevenLabs (orange) | Google (multi-color cool) |

If both tools have similar temperature colors, describe one as the warm side and shift the other's glow to be cooler for the split-lighting effect.
