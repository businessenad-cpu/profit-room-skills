# Style E — Whiteboard + Face

## Description
Person centered in the foreground (chest-up, big smile). A real whiteboard fills the background — white surface with hand-drawn marker-style icons and dashed arrows showing a system or workflow. The Claude pixel mascot appears as one drawn element. A full-width white bar at the bottom has two-color heavy typography. Dark navy background outside the whiteboard. Best for explainer/system walkthroughs.

## Placeholder Guide

### [INSERT WHITEBOARD ELEMENTS]
Describe 4-6 simple drawn icons + the Claude mascot. Think about what concepts the video covers and represent each as a simple sketch. Always include connection arrows.

Example fill for a "Claude agents" video:
> Top-left: a simple drawn house with a checkmark (real estate use case). Top-center: a clipboard checklist. Top-right: the Claude pixel mascot (orange-brown blocky robot face, two square black eyes, rectangular body). Center-right: a stethoscope with a question mark (medical). Bottom-right: three chef hats (cooking). Bottom-center: stacked papers with a broom (cleanup tasks). Bottom-left: a folded map with a location pin. Dashed curved arrows connect all elements flowing clockwise around the Claude mascot at top-right, suggesting it powers all use cases.

### Bottom bar: [ACCENT WORD] + [POWER WORD(S)]
Two-word or three-word split:
- "CLAUDE" + "BLUEPRINT"
- "YOUR" + "AI SYSTEM"
- "STOP" + "PROMPTING"
- "THE" + "4 LEVELS"
- Keep total to 2-3 words. Orange word is the brand/tension word. Black word is the subject/noun.

## When to Use
- System or workflow explainer videos
- "How it works" walkthroughs
- Multi-use-case showcase videos
- Agent or automation system demonstrations
- Videos with a teaching/education angle
- Works well with: The Formula, The Insider, Feature Name Only title formats

## Notes
- A bright-colored top can compete with whiteboard elements — specify a neutral top (grey, cream, white tee) in the prompt for this style
- The Claude pixel mascot on the whiteboard signals "this is about Claude" without needing to write it
- The bottom text bar handles all branding/title — no other text overlay needed
