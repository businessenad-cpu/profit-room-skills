# Style G — Word Provocation

## Description
Extreme close-up OR dark portrait of you. 2-4 words only — no charts, no props, no logos. The expression matches the copy tone exactly. Dark or plain background only. Always a command, a provocation, or a paradox. Period at the end is optional but lands like a punch.

Example short-copy provocations: HAVE NO SHAME, REACT TO NOTHING, NOBODY CARES, THANK ME LATER, START OVER

## Full Prompt Template

```
A photorealistic, cinematic, high-resolution YouTube thumbnail. WIDE 16:9 — never portrait.

**Person:** Use Image 1 for exact likeness — match the person's hair, facial hair, and glasses from your reference photo. [INSERT CLOTHING — e.g., hoodie, t-shirt, dark shirt]. [INSERT EXPRESSION — see Expression Guide below]. Upper body visible, chest-up. Person fills roughly half the frame — not extreme close-up.

**Framing:** [INSERT FRAMING — e.g., "Centered, slightly left-of-center, direct gaze into camera" OR "Three-quarter shot, looking slightly downward, candid — not at camera" OR "Head-on, arms crossed, direct stare"].

**Background:** Dark studio or environmental background — always has some context, never plain flat charcoal. Options:
- Dark room with visible (but blurred) lighting equipment, curtains, or studio gear in the background
- Blurred office or room — recognizable as a space but not distracting
- Private jet interior, gym, or workspace if it fits the topic
Background must be DARK overall. No bright backgrounds. The environment is secondary to the face — 15-20% visible, just enough to feel grounded.

**Lighting:** [INSERT LIGHTING — e.g., "Soft even studio light, slight rim on edges" OR "Warm side light, slight shadow on one side" OR "Hard dramatic side lighting"].

**Typography — BOTTOM OF FRAME, full-width (signature bottom placement):**
Text sits directly over the image at the bottom — NO background bar, NO panel, NO box behind it.
Just the text itself with an extremely heavy drop shadow for legibility against the background.
[INSERT TEXT] in bold heavy sans-serif, left-aligned or centered.
[INSERT COLOR ACCENT WORD if any — accent color is RED or YELLOW only (not orange — orange is for banners/I-style):
- Red = aggressive, provocative: "HAVE NO" white + "SHAME" red
- Yellow = reward, invitation: "your time is" white + "limited." yellow
Pick based on tone. All white is also valid when the words carry all the weight.]
Can be ALL CAPS or sentence case — both are valid. Period at end optional but adds punch.
Drop shadow must be very strong — thick, dark, slightly blurred — so the text pops off any background color.
Appears ONCE ONLY. Do not add any background element behind the text.

**NO other elements.** No logos, no charts, no icons, no objects. Face, environment, and bottom text bar only.

**CRITICAL:** Wide 16:9. Person dominant. Text as full-width bottom bar. Environmental dark background. Nothing else.
```

## Expression Guide (match to copy tone)

| Copy tone | Expression | Example |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| Command | Calm, neutral, direct eye contact | "REACT TO NOTHING" |
| Provocation | Slight smirk, one eyebrow raised | "HAVE NO SHAME" |
| Paradox | Thoughtful, slight head tilt | "Nobody Cares About You." |
| Secret/reward | Knowing look, hint of smile | "Thank Me Later" |
| Challenge | Arms crossed, direct stare | "STOP DOING THIS" |
| Intensity | Hard stare, jaw set, no smile | "YOU'RE WASTING IT" |

## Typography Options

**White + Red:** One word or phrase in red for maximum contrast on dark background. Most aggressive.
**White + Yellow:** Softer than red, works for reward/invitation framing. A good default.
**All white:** Cleanest. Works when the words alone carry all the weight.

## When to Use
- Opinion/take videos — you have a strong position
- Personal philosophy content
- Reaction/commentary videos
- Identity-level content (not tutorial content)
- Pairing: The Accusation, The Verdict title formats from title_rules.md

## Key Rules
- NEVER use this style for tutorial/how-to content — it will feel dishonest
- The expression must match the words exactly — mismatch kills the click
- Period at end = provocation ("Nobody Cares About You.") | No period = command ("STOP DOING THIS")
- Dark background is non-negotiable — bright backgrounds kill the intensity
